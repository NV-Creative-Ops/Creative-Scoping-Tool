Project Details

Video & Motion sizing note: Small (S) is reserved for social edits only — re-cutting existing footage for social platforms. Any new production, scripted video, or motion work beyond a social cut lands at M or higher. The majority of video requests fall into L or XL territory.
Photography sizing note: Small (S) applies to retouching and light Photoshop updates only. Any work requiring new photography — even a minimal shoot — will exceed Small. The majority of photography requests fall into M, L, or XL territory.
3D & Animation sizing note: Templates exist at L and XL only. 3D animation and render work almost always requires significant production time, modeling, and rendering. This category starts at L — if your scope feels like L, size up to XL when in doubt.
Copy & Content sizing note: Templates are available for XS, S, and M. Larger copy requests (L–XL integrated campaigns, GTM playbooks) typically pair with a Campaign & Brand project rather than standing alone.
Social & Digital sizing note: Social Media templates run XS through M. Larger social campaign packages (integrated paid + organic, launch suites) are scoped as L and use Campaign & Brand templates. This category caps at L.
